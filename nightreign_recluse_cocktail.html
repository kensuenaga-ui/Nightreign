System How Magic Cocktail Works
Residue Collection
Recluse's passive (Elemental Defense) absorbs affinity from elemental damage dealt to anything with a health bar. Each absorption stores one charge: Magic, Fire, Lightning, or Holy. Each collection also restores 20 FP flat regardless of stats.
Cocktail Trigger
Collect 3 residues, then cast with Triangle + L2. The spell cast is determined by the combination of the 3 most recently collected residues. The order of collection does not matter — only which elements were collected.
Quantity Rule
For 2+1 combinations, it does not matter which element has 2 and which has 1. 2×Magic + 1×Fire = 1×Magic + 2×Fire — both produce the same spell. Only the element types present matter.
Relic Scaling
All cocktails count as Sorceries. "Improved Sorceries" on deep relics boosts all cocktail damage. "Improved Affinity Attack Power" also boosts all. Specific boosts (e.g. Fire Attack Power Up) only affect cocktails dealing that element type.
Multiplayer
Residues are shared between all Recluse players in a run — if one collects a residue, it cannot be collected by the other. Residues can also be collected from teammates and from near-death allies.
Spell Tiers
Triple combos (all 3 elements different) are consistently the most powerful. Dual combos (2+1) are the workhorses — easy to engineer and reliable. Pure combos (3×same) are mostly early-game or niche.
Quick key Magic = Lingering Fire = Area of Effect Lightning = Melee / Dash Holy = Support Mix elements by their roles to predict the output intuitively.

Pure Pure Cocktails — 3× Same Element

Simplest to obtain, generally weakest in late game. Primarily utility or FP management tools.

Pure Magic — Magic Wisp C
"Enwreathe an enemy in magic that deals damage for some time, encircling and attacking the target."
MechanicTracking wisp attaches to a single enemy and pulses magic damage at intervals. First hit staggers most humanoid enemies. Wisp persists ~10s regardless of range.
DamageMagic Moderate total across 4 hits
Duration~10 seconds, 4 damage pulses
Team UseEarly-game FP regen tool. Wisp creates residue opportunities while active. Target death mid-wisp wastes remaining hits.
RiskLow damage at endgame. Don't prioritise farming pure Magic if better combos are available.
† ~88 base + ~55 per pulse × 4 hits (level 15, unmodified)
Pure Fire — Fire Whirlwind C
"Throw a whirlwind of flame. Flame spreads across a small area upon contact and burns for some time."
MechanicShort-range thrown AoE. Leaves a lingering fire area on impact covering roughly 3–4 player widths. Burn persists after the initial hit.
DamageFire Moderate — spread across duration
DurationBurns for 8 seconds
Team UseGood in confined corridors. Boss-phase utility against slow enemies. Outclassed by Fire Wisp (M+F) for single targets.
RiskShort cast range. Agile bosses leave the AoE immediately.
† ~210 fire total over duration (level 15, unmodified)
Pure Lightning — Bloodhound's Step D
"Enwreathe oneself in lightning, bolstering dodging actions."
MechanicReplaces Recluse's dodge roll with Bloodhound's Step for 20 seconds. Also fires a brief AoE lightning pulse on cast. Bloodhound's Step has longer iframes but different timing from her normal dodge.
DamageLightning ~120 on cast AoE only
Duration20 seconds
Team UseNiche defensive option. The dodge buff actively disrupts muscle memory — you may roll-catch yourself by dodging too early. Most experienced Recluse players avoid intentionally farming this.
RiskDisrupts normal roll timing. Lightning is hard to farm in early runs.
† ~120 lightning AoE on cast (level 15, unmodified)
Pure Holy — Divine Shield B
"Enwreathe oneself in holy light, bolstering poise and increasing all damage negation by 10%. Enemy attacks that cause minor stagger can instead be poised through."
Mechanic+10% damage negation from all sources. Poise buff allows poising through minor staggers. Longest pure buff at 30 seconds. Worth pre-casting before high-damage boss phases.
DamageSelf-buff None — defensive only
Duration30 seconds — longest pure buff duration
Team UseStrong before lethal boss combos. Holy residue is the hardest to farm — reserve for high-value moments. Best on Recluse solo since it only affects the caster.
RiskHoly residue requires holy elemental damage on your team's kit, which is uncommon in early runs.
† No damage — defensive buff only
Dual Dual Cocktails — 2+1 Element Mix

The consistent backbone of Recluse's kit. Engineering specific dual combos is realistic in most runs.

Magic + Fire — Fire Wisp B
"Launch a wraith flame that pursues enemies."
MechanicHoming fire projectile that tracks a single target dealing fire damage over ~10 seconds, then detonates for additional fire burst. Pursues even at long range.
DamageFire Good — DoT + detonation
DurationPursues ~10s, then explodes
Team UseSet-and-forget target damage. Excellent for maintaining DPS while repositioning or collecting new residues. Magic+Fire is one of the easiest combos to farm on any boss.
RiskDestroyed if target dies before detonation. Slow projectile vs. fast-moving bosses means occasional misses.
† ~390 fire total (DoT + detonation, level 15, unmodified)
Magic + Lightning — Carian Sword S
"Execute broad forward slash with magical blade. Hold to move with sword drawn."
MechanicConjures a Carian blade and fires a Carian Slicer-style slash. Hold to walk with sword drawn and extend the timing/range. Fast cast. Staggers most humanoid enemies and medium enemies. Community name: Pocket Moonveil.
DamageMagic High — best raw damage for a dual combo
DurationInstant / holdable
Team UseBest dual combo. Fast enough to use aggressively. Stagger utility is significant. Farm Magic+Lightning whenever a lightning weapon or Recluse's starting sorceries are active on the team.
RiskRequires Lightning on the team — less reliable solo. No real downsides otherwise.
† ~552 magic (level 15, unmodified)
Magic + Holy — FP Veil A
"Cover nearby area with a magical veil, fully conserving FP for self and allies for a short time."
MechanicGrants infinite FP to Recluse and all nearby allies for 8 seconds. Does not require maintaining position during the window — allies can move freely once the veil activates.
DamageSupport None — team buff only
Duration8 seconds — sufficient for a full heavy spell rotation
Team UseEnables Recluse to dump expensive sorceries freely. Allows Revenant to spam seal incantations. Benefits Scholar, Undertaker with seals in-run. Cast at the start of a lethal boss phase for maximum spell output.
RiskHoly residue is scarce. Cast endlag eats slightly into the window. Don't use when FP is naturally high.
† No damage output

MechanicRecluse transforms into a fireball and dashes forward. Lightning strikes where she lands. Brief partial invulnerability during dash (inconsistently reported). Deposits Recluse inside melee range on landing.

Fire + Holy — Warming Torch A
"Raise a torch for a limited time that raises max HP and reduces status ailment gauge buildup for nearby allies, and lowers max HP of nearby enemies."
Mechanic+30% max HP to self and nearby allies. Actively cures status ailments on allies in range. Lowers enemy max HP within range. Torch lasts 15s — allies do NOT need to stay near it. HP buff persists 60s from last moment in range (up to 75s total).

"Weave a layer of lightning that parries enemy attacks for a short time."
MechanicActs as a 100% physical damage reduction auto-parry for 15 seconds. Also deflects non-AoE spell projectiles. Cannot be guard-broken. Physical attacks that cannot normally be parried still deal 0 damage (absorbed as stamina cost instead).
DamageDefensive None
Duration15 seconds of auto-parry coverage
Team UseExceptional panic button during high-aggression boss phases when Recluse is targeted. Best used proactively before a known lethal combo rather than reactively. 15 seconds is a long window.
RiskRear attacks and grabs still connect. Lightning+Holy combination is difficult to control. Does not protect teammates.
† No damage — defensive shield only
Triple Triple Cocktails — All 3 Elements Different

The most powerful and complex cocktails. Requires team coordination to farm three different element types. Worth engineering for Nightlord encounters.

Magic + Fire + Lightning — Gravity Orb S
"Conjure a frontward exploding gravity orb. Gravity orb pulls enemies inward, expands, and explodes, inflicting heavy damage."
MechanicGravity orb launches forward, actively pulling enemies inward, then detonates for massive magic AoE. Community name: Hollow Purple. Gravity-type damage is primarily physical in bucket. Deals 60 revive damage in AoE — can revive 1-bar downed allies in a single cast. Terra Magica boosts this cocktail.
DamageMagic Very High — best practical triple combo
DurationInstant detonation — no delay
Team UseBest practical triple combo despite not being the highest raw damage. Pull mechanic guarantees enemies can't escape. Revive utility makes it a team-wide lifeline. No delayed timing — immediately reliable. Works with Terra Magica and sorcery relics.
RiskForward cast requires facing target. Long cast animation vulnerable to interruption. No AoE revive if the orb doesn't reach allies.
† ~870 magic (pull + detonation combined, level 15, unmodified)
Magic + Fire + Holy — Fire Breath S
"Spew a mixed affinity breath that inflicts fire damage upon enemies, and restores HP and FP of allies within contact."
MechanicWide channeled breath attack. Simultaneous damage to enemies and HP + FP restoration to nearby allies during the channel. Can free-aim during cast. Breath travels in a slight upward arc — free-aim recommended for ground-level targets.
DamageFire Very High — second highest total damage
DurationChanneled ~2–3 seconds
Team UseUnique dual-output: second-highest damage cocktail in the game AND simultaneously restores ally HP+FP. Exceptional in sustained fights — team heals while boss takes massive damage. Cosplay Medic from TF2.
RiskRecluse fully vulnerable during channel. Interruption = partial damage. Arc trajectory struggles against elevated enemies.
† ~1,748 fire total at full channel duration (level 15, unmodified)
Magic + Lightning + Holy — Ice Storm B
"Create a cold storm with a frostbite buildup area effect and retreat inside ice block, gaining invulnerability."
MechanicRecluse casts a frostbite AoE storm then encases herself in an ice block for 8 seconds of full invulnerability. Deals magic damage and builds frostbite in the surrounding area. Recluse is completely immobile inside the ice block — cannot move, heal, or cast.
DamageMagic Low-moderate + frostbite buildup
Duration8 seconds invulnerable (fully immobile)
Team UseNiche invulnerability window for surviving unavoidable boss AoEs. Excellent against Gladius (damage triples when he splits, and frostbite buildup can proc). Teammates attack freely while Recluse is protected. Must be timed precisely.
RiskRecluse is a statue for 8 seconds. Cannot help allies, cannot reposition. Wrong timing = completely wasted window.
† ~400 magic + frostbite buildup (level 15, unmodified)
Fire + Lightning + Holy — Lightning Rod A
"Create a lightning rod and thrust forward into terrain. White lightning strikes the rod after a short delay."
MechanicRecluse thrusts a stake into the ground (initial hit: 467 lightning). After ~5 seconds, a massive white lightning strike detonates on the rod (1,637 lightning). Total: 2,104 lightning — highest raw damage cocktail. Known targeting bug: misfires on downhill terrain.
DamageLightning Highest raw — 2,104 total when setup lands
DurationRod stays ~5 seconds before detonation
Team UseS-tier against stationary or staggered targets. Team should hold aggro during the delay. Outstanding in phase transitions where the boss briefly pauses. Kills lightweight adds like Libra summons instantly.
RiskEnemy moving out of rod range during delay = 467 damage instead of 2,104. Cast interruption = total loss. Terrain bug on slopes. Requires all three non-magic elements on the team.
† 467 (initial thrust) + 1,637 (detonation) = 2,104 lightning total (level 15, unmodified) — highest raw damage of any cocktail

2FFire Breath (M+F+H)— Full channel duration

4MCarian Sword (M+L)— Best dual combo
5FFire Wisp (M+F)— Reliable, low effort
FP Loop Technique
Maintain a cocktail loop using Fire Wisp and Carian Sword. Cast Fire Wisp, absorb its residues as it hits (Fire + Magic), then build toward Carian Sword. The Fire Wisp generates residue opportunities while active, effectively self-funding. Use Glintstone Pebble as a low-cost residue extender.
Team Element Coordination
Coordinate with teammates on elemental weapon types before boss fights. A lightning weapon on Executor or Raider gives Recluse reliable Lightning access. Holy weapons are rare — plan for them or accept that Holy combos are situational. Elemental throwable pots are a valid supplementary source.
Terra Magica Interaction
Terra Magica boosts Pure Magic cocktail output but does NOT boost all cocktails. Only cocktails classified as general sorceries in the Sorcery bucket benefit — verify per combo. "Improved Sorceries" on deep relics reliably boosts everything. Specific elemental attack-up relics only boost their matching element type.
Pre-Loading for Boss Phases
Cocktails can be engineered in advance before a fight starts — farm the needed residues during Day 2 traversal, then carry them into the Nightlord gate. Ideal pre-load: Gravity Orb (M+F+L) stored for the opening burst, then farm freely during Phase 1 for a second cast in Phase 2.
Revive Mechanic (Gravity Orb)
Gravity Orb deals 60 revive damage in its AoE on detonation. This is enough to instantly revive 1-bar downed allies caught in the blast. In trio runs with multiple downed teammates, a well-placed Gravity Orb can simultaneously deal boss damage and perform a group revive — prioritise this in emergencies.
