Overview Rise Types

The first thing to determine when approaching any rise is which type it is. This dictates everything else.

Type 1 · Outside Seal
Closed Door
The entrance door has a visible magical seal on it. The crest is hidden somewhere outside the tower in the surrounding area.
→ Follow Outside Puzzle protocol
Type 2 · No Seal
Fog Door Only
The door has fog but no seal icon on it. This is an illusory barrier — walk directly through it. No puzzle required.
→ Walk through, climb to top
Type 3 · Inside Seal
Open Ground Floor
The ground floor entrance is freely accessible. The seal blocks the second floor. The crest is hidden inside the tower on the first floor.
→ Follow Inside Puzzle protocol
Type 4 · No Puzzle
Spirit Spring Rise
The door appears sealed but spirit springs are visible nearby. There is no crest to find. Ride the spirit spring to vault directly to the top.
→ Use spring to bypass
Type 5 · Multi-Crest
Ruined Rise
Covered in crystals. Unique map icon. Requires finding and breaking multiple crests. Some crests explode on destruction. Richer rewards inside.
→ See Ruined Rise section
Mechanics Crest Basics

Before solving any puzzle, know what you are looking for and how to break it.

What it is
A glowing blue sigil — a light circle with esoteric symbols inside. Sometimes visible directly, sometimes hidden or invisible until triggered.
How to break
Stand inside or on the crest and perform a dodge roll, or simply attack it with any weapon. You will hear a shattering sound and all players receive a message that the rise is unlocked.
Explosions
Some crests in Ruined Rises explode when destroyed. Back off or use a ranged hit. Standard rise crests do not explode.
Lock-on tip
Spam the lock-on button when scanning — targetable elements like running stumps and hidden statues will snap your camera to them before you can see them visually.
After opening
Enter the tower and use the crystal stacks to parkour upward to the second floor. The loot chamber is at the top. Crystals are the scaffolding — there are no stairs.
Outside Ground Floor Sealed — Crest is Outside

When the entrance door is sealed, the crest is always outside. The environmental clue tells you exactly where to look.

01
Illusory Door
How to identify
The door appears sealed with a blue barrier but has no crest symbol on it — the center of the door is blank.
Solution
Walk straight through it. No crest to find, no attack needed. The seal is an illusion with no lock.
02
Crest Above the Door
How to identify
Looking at the sealed entrance, the glowing blue crest is visible floating directly above the doorway archway.
Solution
Hit it with a ranged attack (bow, incantation) or jump-attack up to it. The crest is in plain sight — it just requires elevation to reach.
03
Imp Statue
How to identify
A stone imp statue (the same type found outside Evergaols) stands near the base of the entrance steps, holding a book.
Solution
Attack the statue. It dispels as an illusion and reveals the crest, which you then break to unlock the seal.
Variant: A small podium or piece of furniture sits by the steps instead of an imp. Same solution — attack it.
04
Trembling Trees
How to identify
A cluster of trees on one side of the rise (usually the left or clockwise side) shakes and shudders erratically as if blown by a fierce, localised wind — but the rest of the environment is still.
Solution
Run up to the base of the shaking trees and attack them, or run through their center. The crest will be revealed at their base.
05
Dead Trees
How to identify
Bare, leafless dead tree trunks stand near the rise. Unlike live trees, they have no foliage. They are static but destructible.
Solution
Attack and destroy the dead trees. The crest will appear once the illusion is dispelled. Hit all dead trees if the first one does not reveal it immediately.
06
Running Tree Stump
How to identify
A tree stump, usually near the pond on the counterclockwise side of the tower, begins to slide away from you as you approach. This is the rarest and trickiest outside puzzle.
Solution
Chase it down and attack it before it escapes too far. Spam lock-on to track it. Once you hit it, it dispels and reveals the crest.
Tip: approach from the far side to cut off its escape path. It moves slowly enough to catch on foot.
07
Pond Reflection
How to identify
There is a pool of water nearby (counterclockwise / left side of the tower). When you look into the water, you can see a faint reflection of the glowing blue crest — but nothing is visible above the surface where the crest should be.
Solution
Using the reflection as a guide, jump or dodge-roll into the air at the location where the invisible crest floats above the water. You can also attack the air at that position. The crest will shatter.
In Ruined Rises, the pond crest may instead be on the wall above the pond — look up at the tower wall, not the water.
08
Tower Extension / Fake Building
How to identify
A structure — a small building, archway, or wall section — appears to be attached to the base of the tower but clearly does not belong architecturally. It looks out of place or newer than the rest.
Solution
Attack and destroy the fake structure. It is an illusion. Breaking it reveals the hidden crest underneath or inside it.
09
Invisible Outer Crest
How to identify
No environmental clue is visible at all. This is the rarest and most frustrating variant. There may be a faint audio hum or shimmer but nothing clearly targetable. Usually found on the right outer wall, above the waterline.
Solution
Climb the ledge on the right side of the tower exterior and sweep the wall with attacks or AOE skills (Storm Stomp, Storm Caller). Listen for the crest's faint audio pulse to narrow the position. Any wide-area skill will trigger it if you are close enough.
This may also be triggered by other players opening the contraption in a nearby phantom run — watch for the "Another player has opened the rise" message.
Inside Ground Floor Open — Crest is Inside

When the entrance is open (or fog-only), the crest is on the first floor inside. The seal blocks the second floor. Check specific locations immediately upon entering.

I–1
Unlit Candle
How to identify
Upon entering, immediately look up and to your left at the pillar or wall near the door. One of the candles on the wall is unlit — dark where the others glow.
Solution
Attack the unlit candle to ignite it. When the flame takes hold, the crest appears nearby. Break the crest to unseal the second floor.
Use a jump attack or ranged hit — the candle is typically above head height on the pillar.
I–2
Missing / Invisible Statue
How to identify
Look to the right of the crystal scaffold as you enter. There should be a statue but it is absent — a blank wall or empty pedestal where something should stand.
Solution
Attack the empty space or the blank wall in the far-right corner where the statue should be. The invisible statue is an illusion — hitting it dispels it and reveals the crest.
I–3
Missing Torch Above Door
How to identify
The main entrance door is open. Look at the wall directly above the open doorway from inside. A torch bracket is empty — a gap in the sconces where light should be.
Solution
Climb or jump up to the wall above the entrance and attack the empty torch bracket. This reveals the crest at that location.
I–4
Second Floor Crest
How to identify
None of the above inside clues are present. The crest is not on the ground floor at all — it is on the second floor interior, usually visible when you look behind you after climbing up.
Solution
Climb the crystals to the second floor. When you arrive, turn around and look behind you — the crest is often on the wall above the entry point. Jump-attack to break it.
Special Ruined Rise — Multi-Crest

Ruined Rises have a unique crystal-covered icon on the map and require finding multiple crests before the tower unlocks. They contain richer loot — but cost more time and split attention.

R–1
Behind the Rise
How to identify
One crest is placed directly behind the tower — the side opposite the entrance. It may be partially visible.
Solution
Circle the tower and attack the crest on the rear wall.
⚠ May explode on destruction — back off after hitting it.
R–2
Above the Pond (Wall)
How to identify
Unlike the standard pond reflection (where the crest floats above the water), in Ruined Rises the crest is instead on the tower wall above the pond — a quarter turn counterclockwise from the entrance.
Solution
Look up at the tower wall on the pond side. Climb the ledge or use a ranged attack to hit the wall-mounted crest.
⚠ May explode on destruction.
R–3
Standard Outside Variants
How to identify
Ruined Rises also draw from the same outside puzzle pool — imp statues, trembling trees, pond reflection, etc. — for their remaining crests.
Solution
Solve each crest as you would for a standard rise. All crests must be found and destroyed before the tower fully unlocks.
Three crests total is standard. Find the two ruined-specific ones (behind + pond wall) first, then scan for any remaining standard variant.
Strategy Fastest Clear — Decision Flow & Scan Protocol

The goal is to identify the rise type and locate the crest in a single pass with no backtracking. Follow this order on every run.

Step 0 — Read the Rise Before You Land
Are spirit springs visible near the rise?
Yes Use the spirit spring to vault to the top. Puzzle is irrelevant — this is a Spirit Spring Rise. Skip everything else.
No Continue to Step 1.
Does the entrance door have a visible seal on it?
Yes Crest is outside. Follow the Outside Scan Protocol below.
Fog only, no seal Walk straight through — illusory door. No puzzle. Climb to top.
Open Ground floor is accessible. Crest is inside. Follow the Inside Scan Protocol below.
Is the map icon showing crystal/ruin decoration?
Yes Ruined Rise — multiple crests required. See Ruined Rise scan below.
No Standard rise — single crest.
Outside Scan Protocol (Sealed Door)
01
Instant check — look at the door
Is the crest floating above the doorway? Shoot or jump-attack immediately. Done in 3 seconds.
02
Instant check — base of steps
Imp statue or podium at the bottom of the entrance steps? Attack it. Done in 3 seconds.
03
Scan left/pond side
Is there a pond? Spam lock-on while walking toward it — targetable running stump will snap your camera to it. If no stump, look at the water surface for a reflection glow.
04
Scan surrounding trees
Any trees shaking violently (trembling)? Attack them. Any dead bare trunks standing nearby? Attack them.
05
Check tower body
Any structure attached to the base that looks out of place? Destroy it. Then check right outer wall — AOE the area above the waterline.
06
Last resort — AOE sweep
Still nothing? Circumnavigate and spam Storm Stomp or any AOE skill around the base. Listen for the shattering sound. Invisible crest somewhere on the exterior wall.
Inside Scan Protocol (Open Door)
01
Enter — immediately look left
Left wall / pillar near the door: is any candle unlit? Hit it immediately. This is the most common inside puzzle.
02
Look right — far corner
Is a statue missing on the right side of the scaffold? Attack the blank space or wall. Invisible statue is there.
03
Check above the entrance
Look back at the wall above the door from inside. Is a torch bracket empty? Climb and hit it.
04
Climb to second floor
Nothing on the first floor? Climb the crystals and turn around at the top — the crest is on the wall behind you at second-floor level.
Ruined Rise — Efficient Order
01
Circle the tower first
Go directly behind the rise — hit the rear wall crest. Step back in case it explodes.
02
Pond side wall
Look up at the tower wall above the pond — hit the wall-mounted crest with ranged or jump attack.
03
Remaining standard scan
Apply the outside scan protocol for any remaining crests. Typically one more standard variant remains.
Tips Practical Notes
Route it early. Rises near the map edge — path through them on Day 1 before the tide circle closes in. Don't make a dedicated trip; fold it into your route to Forts or Flask upgrades.
Ironeye solves outside puzzles fastest. A bow trivially hits crests above doorways and wall-mounted crests in Ruined Rises without any platforming required.
The lock-on button is your detector. Before scanning visually, spam the lock-on while walking around the tower. Any targetable illusion — stump, statue, podium — will be flagged even if you can't see it.
Split the search. With a three-person team, one checks the front (door/above door/imp), one checks the pond side (stump/reflection), one checks the rear and trees. A coordinated team solves any rise in under 20 seconds.
Ruined Rises: exploding crests. Hit and immediately back away. Stand behind cover or use ranged. The detonation is not severe but enough to chunk HP on an already-damaged team.
Phantom assists. If another player in a nearby parallel run opens their own rise contraption, you may receive a message that your rise is also unlocked. Check it immediately before assuming the puzzle needs solving.
Worth it for everyone, not just casters. Rises can drop talismans, rings, and non-magical armaments. Do not skip them because your team has no Recluse or Revenant — the loot pool includes melee and general-purpose items.
Crystal parkour. Once inside, the crystals are the stairs. There are no conventional staircases. Jump on the lowest crystal cluster and bounce upward to the platform. It is faster than it looks the first time.
